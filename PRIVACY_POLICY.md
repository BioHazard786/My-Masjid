# Privacy Policy for My Masjid

**Last Updated: October 14, 2025**

## Introduction

My Masjid ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (securely encrypted)
- **Mosque Information**: If you're a mosque administrator, we collect mosque details including name, address, and contact information
- **Prayer Time Updates**: When you update prayer times for your mosque

### Information Automatically Collected

- **Device Information**: We collect a unique anonymous device identifier to enable personalized features
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you use the app, including search queries for mosques
- **Technical Data**: App version, device type, operating system, and crash reports to improve app stability

### Location and Notification Data

- **Push Notification Tokens**: We collect Expo push tokens to send you prayer time updates and mosque notifications
- **Pinned Mosques**: Information about which mosques you've saved or pinned for quick access
- **Language Preferences**: Your preferred language (English, Hindi, or Urdu) for notifications and app content

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### Core Functionality

- Provide access to mosque information and prayer times
- Enable mosque search and discovery features
- Allow mosque administrators to manage their mosque profiles and prayer schedules
- Send push notifications about prayer time updates

### Service Improvement

- Analyze usage patterns to improve app performance and user experience
- Provide multilingual support based on your language preference
- Ensure app stability and fix technical issues

### Communication

- Send important service announcements
- Provide customer support when requested
- Notify you about prayer time changes for your pinned mosques

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

### With Your Consent

We will share information when you explicitly agree to such sharing.

### Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in:

- Cloud hosting and data storage (Cloudflare Workers)
- Push notification services (Expo Push Notifications)
- Translation services for multilingual content

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government regulation.

### Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

## Data Storage and Security

### Security Measures

- All passwords are securely hashed using industry-standard encryption
- API communications are secured with HTTPS encryption
- User sessions are managed with secure authentication tokens
- Database access is restricted and monitored

### Data Retention

- Account information is retained while your account is active
- Anonymous device identifiers and pinned mosque data are retained to provide personalized services
- Push notification tokens are stored only as long as you have the app installed
- We automatically delete inactive accounts after 2 years of no activity

### International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in servers located outside your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers.

## Your Privacy Rights

### Access and Control

- **View Your Data**: You can view your account information and pinned mosques within the app
- **Update Information**: You can modify your account details and prayer times (if you're a mosque administrator)
- **Delete Account**: You can delete your account, which will remove all associated personal data

### Notification Preferences

- **Manage Notifications**: You can enable or disable push notifications through your device settings
- **Language Preferences**: You can change your preferred language for notifications and app content

### Data Portability

Upon request, we can provide you with a copy of your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

## Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will take steps to remove such information.
